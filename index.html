How it works

Three founders. One hour. Every two weeks.

No curriculum. No networking. You’re in until you opt out.

01

Tell me what you're building.

Drop your email. A few quick details — stage, industry, when you’re free — then I’ll follow up personally to learn what you want to bring.

02

I match you with two other founders.

Same stage — founders in their first 2 years. Different industries. You get a calendar invite for a 60-minute Zoom. First session is on me.

03

Bring your question. Return the favour.

One hour, split three ways. Everyone brings something real — a hire, a price, a pivot. You get the truth on yours, then you do the same for theirs.

04

Open your own sessions, anytime.

After session one, founding members stay for AED 139/mo, locked for life. You get a curated match every two weeks — plus the ability to open your own session whenever you need it. Pose a topic, pick a time, and I’ll match you with two other paying founders.

Three founders. Different industries. One hour.
