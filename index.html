How it works

Three founders. One hour. Every two weeks.

Nothing to learn. Nothing to chase. You’re in — until you pause.

01

Tell us what you're building.

A short profile: your industry, your stage, when you're free, and what you want to talk through right now. Two minutes, once.

02

We put you on a call with two other founders.

Every two weeks we match you with two other founders — same stage, different industries. It just lands in your calendar. Busy week? Pause anytime, in one tap.

03

Bring your question, return the favor.

One focused hour, split evenly. Everyone brings something real. You get the truth on yours — then you do the same for theirs.

Want to go deeper?Open your own session on a specific question — other founders at your stage can join.

What a session looks like

Three founders. Different industries. One hour.

Who it's for
